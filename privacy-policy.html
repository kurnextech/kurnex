This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, shared and protected when you use Kurnex. This application is developed and published by Kurnex Tech.

1. Information We Collect

Kurnex stores income, expense, budget, category, note and reminder data locally on your device. These financial records are not sent to our own servers.

We collect or process limited information only when it is necessary to provide, maintain and improve the application. The data may include:

  • Device information: device model, operating system version, device language and general technical information.
  • Usage data: app interactions, features used, performance information and crash logs.
  • Advertising data: ad impressions, clicks, engagement information and advertising identifiers.
  • Purchase data: transaction status related to premium features or in-app purchases.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, messages or payment card details.

2. Advertising

Kurnex may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob.

Advertising partners may collect or process information such as device identifiers, usage information, ad impressions, clicks and engagement data. These services may use cookies or similar technologies where applicable to provide personalized or non-personalized advertisements and measure ad performance.

Users can manage advertising preferences from their device settings, including resetting or limiting the advertising identifier where supported.

3. In-App Purchases

The application may offer premium features through in-app purchases or subscriptions. Purchases are securely processed by Google Play.

We do not collect or store payment card numbers, bank details or billing credentials. Refunds, cancellations and subscription management are handled according to Google Play policies.

4. Notifications and Reminders

Kurnex may use notification permissions to provide payment and note reminders. Reminder information is stored locally on your device. You can disable notification permissions at any time from your device settings.

5. How We Use Information

Collected or processed information may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain the application
  • Improve app performance and stability
  • Understand how users interact with the application
  • Show and measure advertisements
  • Verify premium feature access or purchase status
  • Prevent misuse and protect the application
  • Comply with legal and platform requirements

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent user data. Limited data may be shared only with service providers required for app functionality, advertising, analytics, payment processing, security or legal compliance. These service providers may include Google AdMob and Google Play Billing.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your income, expense, budget, category, note and reminder data is stored locally on your device. You can delete this data by clearing the app data from your device settings or uninstalling the application.

Data processed by third-party services may be retained according to the privacy policies and retention practices of those service providers.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, disclosure or alteration. However, no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

9. User Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under applicable data protection laws, including the right to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of certain data. To make a request, contact us using the email address below.

10. Children’s Privacy

Kurnex is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

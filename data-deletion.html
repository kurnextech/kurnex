This page explains how users of the Kurnex application can delete their local app data, cloud backup data, or request deletion of account-related data. Kurnex is developed and published by Kurnex Tech.

1. Locally Stored App Data

Kurnex can store income, expense, budget, category, note, reminder and app preference data locally on your device. If you use Kurnex without signing in, your app data is stored locally on your device and cloud backup is not used.

You can delete locally stored Kurnex app data by following these steps:

  • Open your device Settings.
  • Go to Apps or Applications.
  • Select Kurnex.
  • Open Storage.
  • Tap Clear Data or Clear Storage.

You can also delete locally stored data by uninstalling the Kurnex application from your device.

2. Cloud Backup Data

If you sign in with Google and use cloud backup or restore features, your Kurnex data may be stored in Firebase/Google cloud services. This may include income and expense records, categories, notes, reminders, budget information, app settings, recurring templates and report archive data.

Premium status, demo counters, purchase entitlement and similar premium access information are not included in normal cloud backup data.

3. Delete Cloud Backup Data Inside the App

If you are signed in with Google, you can delete your Kurnex cloud backup data directly inside the app:

  1. Open the Kurnex app.
  2. Go to the Profile page.
  3. Make sure you are signed in with the Google account whose cloud backup data you want to delete.
  4. Tap Delete all cloud data or Buluttaki tüm verileri sil.
  5. Confirm the deletion request.

This action deletes the Kurnex cloud backup data associated with the signed-in Google account, where available. It does not delete data stored only on your device unless you also clear app data or uninstall the app.

4. Request Data Deletion by Email

If you cannot access the app or cannot delete your cloud backup data from the app, you can request deletion by contacting us:

Email: panter_64@hotmail.com

Please include the following information in your request:

  • Your request: Data deletion request for Kurnex
  • The Google account email address used in Kurnex, if cloud backup was used
  • A short explanation of what data you want deleted

5. Data That Can Be Deleted

The following Kurnex data may be deleted from your device or cloud backup where applicable:

  • Income and expense records
  • Budget information
  • Categories
  • Notes and reminders
  • Recurring templates
  • Report archive data
  • App preferences and settings stored locally or in cloud backup

6. Data That May Be Retained

Some limited information may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, platform requirements, billing records, dispute resolution, or enforcement of our policies.

Premium, purchase, subscription, advertising, analytics, crash reporting and platform-related data may also be processed or retained by third-party service providers according to their own policies.

7. Data Processed by Third-Party Services

Kurnex may use third-party services such as Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication, Firebase/Cloud Firestore, Google AdMob and Google Play Billing where applicable.

Data processed by these services is handled according to their own privacy policies and data retention practices.

8. Processing Time

We will review deletion requests and process them within a reasonable time, subject to verification and applicable legal or platform requirements.

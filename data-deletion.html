This page explains how users of the Kurnex application can delete their data or request data deletion. Kurnex is developed and published by Kurnex Tech.

1. Locally Stored App Data

Kurnex stores income, expense, budget, category, note and reminder data locally on your device. These financial records are not sent to Kurnex Tech servers.

You can delete locally stored Kurnex app data by following these steps:

  • Open your device Settings.
  • Go to Apps or Applications.
  • Select Kurnex.
  • Open Storage.
  • Tap Clear Data or Clear Storage.

You can also delete locally stored data by uninstalling the Kurnex application from your device.

2. Data Deletion Request

If you want to request deletion of data related to Kurnex, you can contact us by email:

Email: panter_64@hotmail.com

Please include the following information in your request:

  • Your request: Data deletion request for Kurnex
  • The email address you use to contact us
  • A short explanation of your request

3. Data That Can Be Deleted

The following app data can be deleted from your device:

  • Income and expense records
  • Budget information
  • Categories
  • Notes
  • Reminder information
  • App preferences stored locally on the device

4. Data Processed by Third-Party Services

Kurnex may use third-party services such as Google AdMob for advertisements and Google Play Billing for premium purchases or subscriptions. Data processed by these services is handled according to their own privacy policies and data retention practices.

Purchase history, subscription status, advertising identifiers, diagnostics or other data processed by Google services may be retained by Google according to Google policies and legal requirements.

5. Data Retention

Locally stored Kurnex data remains on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the application.

If you contact us by email, we may retain your request message only as long as necessary to respond to your request, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes or enforce our policies.
